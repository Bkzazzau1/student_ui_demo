## Why

K-SLAS checks that the person writing an exam is the registered student by
comparing their face with their Face ID on the device. This study measures how
accurate that check is on the laptops and tablets students use, and sets how
strict it must be.

## What we take

About 30 to 40 photos of your face: 7 registration photos, then 3 photos per
session on each study device, in normal light, in harder light and on another
day. You get a study code such as v001; your name is never stored with the
photos.

## How they are used

Only to calculate match scores for this study. A computer compares your
photos with your own and with other volunteers' photos to count how often the
check makes mistakes. They are never used to identify you, in exams, to train
AI, or for anything else.

## Storage and deletion

Photos are stored encrypted on the study devices and on the study computer.
They are not uploaded to the internet or shared outside the study team. They
are deleted within 30 days of the final report, which contains only numbers.

## Your choices

Taking part is voluntary and does not affect your studies or grades. You can
stop at any time and ask for your photos to be deleted, without giving a
reason.

## Optional: spoofing test

With your separate permission, we also show the app a printed photo and a
phone video of you, to check that it is not fooled. These are deleted in the
same way.
